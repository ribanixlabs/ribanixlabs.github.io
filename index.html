How we work

A short list of things we will not compromise on.

01

Private by default

Your rides stay on your phone, your voice stays on your PC, your books are visible only to the partners in them. We collect only what a feature needs.

02

Built for real use

We design for a rider in a ghat with no signal and a trader reconciling at night, not for a demo. Features get tested on real devices before we claim them.

03

Honest about limits

If something is an aid and not a guarantee, or is still a beta, the product says so. Plain language, no dark patterns, no ads in the apps.
